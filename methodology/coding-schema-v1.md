# Coding Schema v1

This coding schema defines the first analytical categories for the AGRIR-Lab component **Greek Legal-Governance Knowledge Infrastructure for AI and Institutional Research**.

## Core Coding Categories

### 1. institutional_actor
Use for ministries, general secretariats, general directorates, directorates, departments, councils, museums, supervisory bodies, public entities, and regional services.

### 2. competence
Use for formal responsibilities, legal mandates, operational tasks, approval powers, supervisory powers, and implementation duties.

### 3. supervision
Use for relations of oversight, ministerial supervision, administrative control, monitoring, evaluation, and accountability chains.

### 4. delegation
Use for delegated authority, transferred signature power, regionalized authority, and competence reassignment.

### 5. permit_or_approval
Use for licenses, authorizations, approvals, permissions, consent procedures, and administrative acts requiring legal authorization.

### 6. museum_governance
Use for museum legal status, governance bodies, boards, directors, staffing, collections management, and institutional autonomy.

### 7. archive_and_documentation
Use for archives, registries, cadastres, documentation systems, digital records, and monument documentation infrastructure.

### 8. anti_trafficking_and_protection
Use for protection of cultural goods, illicit trafficking, export controls, return procedures, documentation of movable heritage, and related safeguards.

### 9. internal_audit
Use for internal audit units, risk assessment, internal control systems, assurance functions, investigations, and accountability mechanisms.

### 10. financial_administration
Use for budgeting, procurement, payroll, supervised entities funding, donations, sponsorships, financial reporting, and administrative support structures.

### 11. resilience_framework
Use for critical entities, continuity, resilience, incident notification, risk preparedness, and cross-cutting governance resilience themes.

### 12. transition_provision
Use for amendments, institutional restructuring, transfers of staff, replacement of units, temporary arrangements, and implementation bridges.

## Notes
- This is the first working version of the coding schema.
- Categories may later be expanded into sub-codes.
- The schema is designed for legal-governance extraction, institutional mapping, and traceability analysis.
