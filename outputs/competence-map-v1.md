# Competence Map v1

This file records the first competence-mapping layer for the AGRIR-Lab component **Greek Legal-Governance Knowledge Infrastructure for AI and Institutional Research**.

## Core Competence Areas

### 1. Organizational Competence
- ministry-level authority
- general secretariat authority
- general directorate authority
- directorate-level responsibility
- department-level implementation roles

### 2. Heritage Protection Competence
- monument protection
- archaeological site protection
- movable heritage protection
- documentation and archival competence
- museum-related competence
- cultural goods control functions

### 3. Delegated Administrative Competence
- delegated signature authority
- regional service competence
- special regional service competence
- approval powers
- permit-related competence

### 4. Internal Oversight Competence
- internal audit functions
- risk-related assessment
- internal control review
- investigation-related competence
- accountability support roles

### 5. Financial and Administrative Competence
- budgeting
- procurement
- payroll
- supervised entities support
- donations and sponsorship administration
- financial reporting

### 6. Governance and Transition Competence
- restructuring authority
- transfer of responsibilities
- transition provisions
- implementation bridges
- inter-institutional coordination

## Current Status
- This is the first competence placeholder map.
- The next version will connect specific legal documents to competence categories.
- Later versions will distinguish central, regional, supervisory, and delegated competences.
