# AGRIR-Lab Public Overview v1

## Title
Greek Legal-Governance Knowledge Infrastructure for AI and Institutional Research

## Overview
This AGRIR-Lab component develops a structured legal-governance corpus focused on the Ministry of Culture in Greece.

The goal is not only to collect legal and administrative texts, but to organize them into a research-ready infrastructure that supports institutional interpretation, competence tracing, governance analysis, and AI-assisted legal research.

## What the current pilot corpus includes
The current pilot corpus has processed ten core documents at first-pass level across two analytical waves.

These documents cover:
- ministry organizational structure
- heritage-protection law
- museum governance reform
- ODAP operational and resource-management logic
- secretariat restructuring
- culture-sports separation
- delegation of competences
- financial and administrative architecture
- internal audit architecture
- audit workflow

## What the component already makes visible
The component already supports first-pass analysis of:
- who governs
- who protects
- who manages resources
- who delegates
- who implements
- who supports
- who controls
- who audits

## Why this matters
The Ministry of Culture governance environment is not built by a single legal text.
It emerges through an interacting stack of organizational decrees, substantive legal rules, reform acts, delegation texts, support architecture texts, and oversight documents.

By processing these materials together, the component helps reveal:
- institutional structure
- competence boundaries
- implementation chains
- support flows
- control flows
- audit architecture

## Research relevance
This component can support:
- legal-governance mapping
- public-sector oversight analysis
- internal control and audit interpretation
- cultural-governance and museum-governance research
- future AI-assisted structured legal knowledge systems

## Current status
The pilot corpus has completed a first full two-wave analytical cycle and now functions as a first research-infrastructure layer for AGRIR-Lab.
