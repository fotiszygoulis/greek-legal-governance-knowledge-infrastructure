# AGRIR-Lab Component Summary v1

## Component Title
Greek Legal-Governance Knowledge Infrastructure for AI and Institutional Research

## Component Scope
This component develops a structured legal-governance corpus focused on the Ministry of Culture in Greece.

Its purpose is to support:
- institutional mapping
- competence tracing
- legal-governance analysis
- public-sector oversight interpretation
- AI-assisted institutional research

## Current Pilot Corpus
The current pilot corpus includes ten core documents processed at first-pass level.

### First Wave
- PD 4/2018
- Law 4858/2021
- Law 4761/2020
- Law 5021/2023
- PD 77/2023
- PD 84/2019

### Second Wave
- FEK B912 Delegation of Competences to Regional Services
- PD 32/2022 Financial and Administrative Architecture
- FEK B204 Internal Audit Unit Establishment
- 2022 Internal Audit Unit Circular

## Analytical Method
The component applies a structured first-pass methodology based on:
- document-level decoding
- coding
- mapping
- cross-document synthesis
- dependency analysis
- chronology analysis
- actor mapping
- competence-cluster mapping
- transition analysis
