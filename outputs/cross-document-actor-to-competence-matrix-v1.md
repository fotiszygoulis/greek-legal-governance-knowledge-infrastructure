## First Actor-to-Competence Insight
The first six documents show that competence is distributed through a multi-actor governance structure rather than a single decision center.
The system combines:
- ministerial authority,
- secretariat-level coordination,
- directorate-level implementation,
- council-based advisory input,
- operational actors,
- museum actors,
- and enforcement actors.
