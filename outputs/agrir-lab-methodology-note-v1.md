# AGRIR-Lab Methodology Note v1

## Scope
This note summarizes the first-pass methodology used to build the Ministry of Culture pilot corpus within the AGRIR-Lab component **Greek Legal-Governance Knowledge Infrastructure for AI and Institutional Research**.

## Methodological Objective
The objective of the pilot corpus is not merely to collect legal texts, but to transform them into a structured legal-governance knowledge infrastructure that can support:
- institutional interpretation
- competence tracing
- governance-layer analysis
- public-sector oversight interpretation
- AI-assisted legal and institutional research

## First-Pass Processing Logic
Each document is processed through a structured first-pass sequence:

### 1. Decoding
The document is read as a legal-governance text and broken into its main analytical nodes.

### 2. Coding
The decoded content is translated into recurring analytical categories such as:
- competence
- supervision
- institutional_actor
- delegation
- financial_administration
- museum_governance
- internal_audit
- control_flow
- support_flow
- oversight_architecture
- transition_provision

### 3. Mapping
The coded material is then translated into:
- institutional nodes
- competence nodes
- actor relations
- governance links
- transition logic
- implementation chains

## Cross-Document Method
After document-level processing, the corpus is analyzed across documents through:
- synthesis
- dependency mapping
- chronology mapping
- layer modeling
- transition mapping
- actor mapping
- competence-cluster mapping
- document-to-cluster comparison
- gap mapping

## Analytical Assumption
The pilot corpus is treated as an interacting legal-governance stack rather than a flat legal archive.
This means that documents are interpreted not in isolation, but in relation to:
- organizational structure
- substantive law
- operational management
- reform acts
- delegation acts
- administrative support architecture
- oversight and audit architecture

## Practical Research Value
This methodology allows the corpus to function as a first research-infrastructure layer that can support:
- institutional mapping of the Ministry of Culture
- legal-governance interpretation
- competence distribution analysis
- delegation tracing
- support-flow and control-flow analysis
- internal audit and oversight interpretation
- future AI-assisted knowledge extraction

## Current Status
The pilot corpus has completed a first full two-wave analytical cycle at first-pass level.

## Next Methodological Uses
The same methodology can be extended to:
- additional Ministry of Culture texts
- downstream implementation acts
- financial execution texts
- internal oversight and audit material
- future visual and computational representations of the corpus
