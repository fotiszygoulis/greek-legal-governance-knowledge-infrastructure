- Law 4858/2021 provides the substantive legal framework of heritage protection.
- Law 4761/2020 provides the ODAP operational and resource-management layer.
- Law 5021/2023 provides museum NPDD reform and wider heritage-governance amendments.
- PD 84/2019 provides dual-secretariat restructuring inside the Ministry.
- PD 77/2023 provides the culture-sports competence-boundary reset.

- PD 32/2022 clarifies the internal financial and administrative support architecture.
- The FEK B204 internal audit text clarifies the institutional placement of internal audit.
- The 2022 internal audit circular clarifies the workflow of internal audit in practice.
