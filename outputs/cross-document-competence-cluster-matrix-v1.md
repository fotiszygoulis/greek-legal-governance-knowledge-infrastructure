## First Competence-Cluster Insight
The first six documents show that competence is not distributed randomly.
It is structured in recurring clusters:
- heritage protection,
- organizational architecture,
- museum governance,
- operational resource management,
- advisory governance,
- minister-centered support,
- and transition/reconfiguration logic.
