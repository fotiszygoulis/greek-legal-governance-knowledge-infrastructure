# Cross-Document Synthesis v1

## Scope
This synthesis note consolidates the first-pass analytical processing of the first six documents already processed in the pilot corpus.

## First Cross-Document Layers
- organizational baseline layer
- substantive heritage-protection layer
- operational resource-management layer
- museum NPDD reform layer
- culture-sports separation layer
- secretariat reconfiguration layer

## First Cross-Document Findings
- PD 4/2018 provides the original organizational baseline of the Ministry.
- Law 4858/2021 provides the substantive legal framework of heritage protection.
- Law 4761/2020 provides the ODAP operational and resource-management layer.
- Law 5021/2023 provides museum NPDD reform and broader governance amendments.
- PD 77/2023 separates culture governance from sports governance.
- PD 84/2019 restructures the Ministry into a dual-secretariat model.

## First Structural Insight
The pilot corpus already shows that the Ministry-level governance environment is not built by a single legal act, but by an interacting stack of:
- organizational decrees
- substantive heritage law
- operational-resource law
- museum-governance reform
- competence-transfer decrees
- secretariat-level reconfiguration acts
