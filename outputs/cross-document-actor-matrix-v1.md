# Cross-Document Actor Matrix v1

## Scope
This file captures the first actor-level matrix across the first six documents processed in the pilot corpus.

## Core Ministerial Actors
- Minister of Culture
- General Secretariat of Culture
- General Secretariat of Contemporary Culture
- ministry-level support structures directly under the Minister

## Heritage-Governance Actors
- General Directorate of Antiquities and Cultural Heritage
- Ephorates of Antiquities
- Ephorate of Underwater Antiquities
- Ephorate of Palaeoanthropology and Speleology
- Acropolis Monuments Conservation Service (YSMA)
- General Directorate of Restoration, Museums and Technical Works

## Council and Advisory Actors
- Central Archaeological Council
- Museum Council
- Central Council of Modern Monuments
- Local Councils of Monuments

## Museum and Operational Actors
- ODAP
- National Archaeological Museum NPDD
- Archaeological Museum of Thessaloniki NPDD
- Archaeological Museum of Heraklion NPDD
- Byzantine and Christian Museum NPDD
- Museum of Byzantine Culture NPDD

## Protection and Enforcement Actors
- Prosecutor for the Protection of Cultural Goods
- state actors relevant to anti-trafficking and return procedures
- approval and supervisory actors relevant to heritage protection

## Support and Control Actors
- General Directorate of Financial Services
- General Directorate of Administrative Support and Electronic Governance
- internal support and reporting-line structures
- minister-centered administrative support layer

## First Actor Logic
The first six documents show that the cultural-governance environment is structured through interacting actor groups:
- ministerial actors,
- heritage-governance actors,
- advisory and council actors,
- museum and operational actors,
- protection and enforcement actors,
- and support/control actors.

## Analytical Use
This actor matrix can support:
- institutional actor identification
- competence tracing by actor
- governance-flow analysis
- later coding of reporting lines and delegation chains
- future visual mapping
