# Second-Wave Synthesis v1

## Scope
This synthesis note consolidates the first-pass analytical processing of the second analytical wave of the pilot corpus.

## Documents Included
- FEK B912 Delegation of Competences to Regional Services
- PD 32/2022 Financial and Administrative Architecture
- FEK B204 Internal Audit Unit Establishment
- 2022 Internal Audit Unit Circular

## Core Analytical Layers of the Second Wave
- delegation layer
- regional implementation layer
- financial and administrative support layer
- internal audit architecture layer
- audit workflow layer
- control-flow layer

## First Second-Wave Findings
- The delegation text clarifies how competence moves from the ministry center to regional services without eliminating central supervision.
- PD 32/2022 clarifies how financial administration, administrative support, support flow, and control are distributed across central and service-level structures.
- The FEK B204 internal audit text clarifies where internal audit is placed inside the ministry architecture and how it relates to leadership, supervised entities, and internal control.
- The 2022 internal audit circular clarifies the implementation-level workflow of internal audit through planning, execution, reporting, and follow-up logic.

## Structural Value of the Second Wave
The second analytical wave adds the operational and oversight dimensions that were less developed in the first wave.
It strengthens the pilot corpus by clarifying:
- who delegates
- who implements
- who supports
- who controls
- who audits

## Combined Insight
The first wave established the structural and substantive legal environment of the Ministry.
The second wave clarifies how that environment operates in practice through:
- delegated authority
- regional execution
- financial and administrative support
- internal oversight
- audit workflow

## Analytical Use
This second-wave synthesis can support:
- delegation tracing

- support-flow interpretation
- control-flow interpretation

- next-stage methodological packaging
