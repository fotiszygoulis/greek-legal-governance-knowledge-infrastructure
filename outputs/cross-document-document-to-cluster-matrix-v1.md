## First Document-to-Cluster Insight
The first six documents do not contribute equally to the same analytical dimensions.
Instead:
- some documents provide organizational structure,
- some provide substantive legal protection,
- some provide operational-resource logic,
- some provide reform and transition logic.

## Analytical Use
This matrix can support:
- document comparison by analytical role
- cluster-based reading of the corpus
- prioritization of future coding work
- later visual diagrams and OSF notes
