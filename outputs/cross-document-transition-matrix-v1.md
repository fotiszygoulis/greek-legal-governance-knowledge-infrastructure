# Cross-Document Transition Matrix v1

## Scope
This file captures the first transition logic across the first six documents processed in the pilot corpus.

## Transition Matrix

### Transition 1
**From**
- PD 4/2018

**To**
- PD 84/2019

**Transition logic**
- movement from the original organizational baseline to a dual-secretariat structure
- internal redistribution of reporting lines
- clearer split between heritage governance and contemporary-culture governance

### Transition 2
**From**
- PD 4/2018
- PD 84/2019

**To**
- Law 4761/2020

**Transition logic**
- addition of the ODAP operational and resource-management layer
- stronger linkage between governance structure and visitor-service/resource exploitation functions

### Transition 3
**From**
- PD 4/2018
- PD 84/2019
- Law 4761/2020

**To**
- Law 4858/2021

**Transition logic**
- consolidation of the substantive legal framework of heritage protection
- stronger legal foundation for monuments, archaeological sites, museums, access, anti-trafficking, and criminal protection

### Transition 4
**From**
- Law 4858/2021
- Law 4761/2020
- PD 84/2019

**To**
- Law 5021/2023

**Transition logic**
- movement toward museum NPDD reform
- restructuring of museum governance
- amendments affecting ODAP and the wider heritage-governance system
- introduction of state indemnity for mobility of cultural goods

### Transition 5
**From**
- PD 4/2018
- PD 84/2019
- Law 4858/2021
- Law 4761/2020
- Law 5021/2023

**To**
- PD 77/2023

**Transition logic**
- final competence-boundary reset
- formal separation of culture governance from sports governance
- renaming of the Ministry of Culture and Sports to Ministry of Culture

## First Transition Insight
The first six documents show that the Ministry-level governance environment evolves through sequential transitions rather than isolated reforms.
The legal-governance system develops through:
- organizational establishment,
- internal reconfiguration,
- operational layering,
- substantive legal consolidation,
- museum reform,
- and competence-boundary reset.

## Analytical Use
This transition matrix can support:
- reform-sequence analysis
- institutional change analysis
- competence-shift tracing
- later diagram production
- OSF methodological explanation
