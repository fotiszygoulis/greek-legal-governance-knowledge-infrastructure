# Cross-Document Dependency Map v1

## Scope
This file captures the first dependency logic across the first six documents processed in the pilot corpus.

## Core Dependency Nodes
- PD 4/2018
- Law 4858/2021
- Law 4761/2020
- Law 5021/2023
- PD 77/2023
- PD 84/2019

## First Dependency Logic

### 1. Organizational Baseline Dependency
- PD 4/2018 functions as the original organizational baseline of the Ministry.
- Later institutional decrees and reform laws are read against the PD 4/2018 structure.

### 2. Substantive Law Dependency
- Law 4858/2021 functions as the substantive heritage-protection framework.
- Museum, protection, access, anti-trafficking, and council-related provisions in later texts depend on the legal environment of Law 4858/2021.

### 3. Operational Resource Dependency
- Law 4761/2020 functions as the ODAP operational and resource-management layer.
- Revenue, visitor-service, retail, publication, property, and support functions connect to the wider ministry and museum environment.

### 4. Museum Reform Dependency
- Law 5021/2023 depends on the pre-existing ministry and heritage-law environment.
- It restructures museum governance through NPDD museums and modifies the wider governance environment linked to Law 4858/2021 and Law 4761/2020.

### 5. Secretariat Reconfiguration Dependency
- PD 84/2019 restructures the Ministry internally through the General Secretariat of Culture and the General Secretariat of Contemporary Culture.
- PD 84/2019 should be read as an internal reporting-line and competence redistribution layer on top of the broader PD 4/2018 architecture.

### 6. Culture-Sports Separation Dependency
- PD 77/2023 redefines the Ministry after the separation of sports governance.
- PD 77/2023 should be read as a later competence-boundary reset affecting how the Ministry is interpreted after the earlier PD 4/2018 and PD 84/2019 arrangements.

## First Dependency Insight
The first six documents do not operate as isolated texts. They form an interacting legal-governance stack where:
- PD 4/2018 provides the organizational baseline,
- Law 4858/2021 provides the substantive protection framework,
- Law 4761/2020 provides the ODAP operational layer,
- Law 5021/2023 provides museum reform and governance amendment logic,
- PD 84/2019 provides internal secretariat restructuring,
- PD 77/2023 provides the final culture-sports competence separation layer.

## Analytical Use
This dependency map can support:
- institutional interpretation
- competence tracing
- transition analysis
- conflict or overlap detection
- later coding of delegation and audit architecture
