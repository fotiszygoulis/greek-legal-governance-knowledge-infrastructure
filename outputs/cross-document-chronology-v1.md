# Cross-Document Chronology v1

## Scope
This file captures the first chronological reading of the first six documents processed in the pilot corpus.

## Chronological Order

### 1. PD 4/2018
- organizational baseline of the Ministry
- initial architecture of central and regional cultural governance

### 2. PD 84/2019
- secretariat-level reconfiguration
- creation of the General Secretariat of Culture
- creation of the General Secretariat of Contemporary Culture
- direct subordination of key support units to the Minister

### 3. Law 4761/2020
- ODAP operational and resource-management layer
- revenue, visitor-service, retail, publication, and property-management logic

### 4. Law 4858/2021
- substantive legal framework of heritage protection
- monuments, archaeological sites, museums, access, anti-trafficking, and criminal protection

### 5. Law 5021/2023
- museum NPDD reform
- state indemnity for mobility of cultural goods
- ODAP amendments
- wider heritage-governance amendments

### 6. PD 77/2023
- formal separation of culture and sports governance
- renaming of the Ministry of Culture and Sports to Ministry of Culture
- transfer of sports governance outside the Ministry

## First Chronological Insight
The first six documents show a layered development of the Ministry-level governance environment:
- first, an organizational baseline is established,
- then internal secretariat restructuring is introduced,
- then operational and resource-management law is added,
- then the substantive heritage code is consolidated,
- then museum-governance reform is introduced,
- and finally the culture-sports competence boundary is reset.

## Analytical Use
This chronology can support:
- sequence-based legal interpretation
- identification of reform waves
- transition analysis
- later institutional timeline visualizations
